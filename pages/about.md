# About Me

Hi, I'm Piero. I'm based in Lima, Peru.

I'm currently completing a Professional Certificate in Data Analytics, covering everything from foundational Python and SQL to machine learning, optimization, and quantitative finance. My focus is on data analytics and applied data science.

One of my main projects throughout the programme is SleepContext, a contextual sleep recommendation engine for university students that connects wearable sleep data to academic and lifestyle variables like deadlines, screen time, and caffeine intake.

Going forward, my goal is to keep building applied data projects that hold up under real scrutiny, not just in a notebook. Outside of that, I'm interested in sports analytics, optimization problems, and learning how systems work end to end.

Feel free to look around, and reach out if something catches your eye.
