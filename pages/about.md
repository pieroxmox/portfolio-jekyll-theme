# About Me

Hi, I'm Piero. I'm based in Lima, Peru, and I spend most of my time working with data, code, and the occasional stubborn bug.

When I'm not in front of a screen, I like digging into how things work, whether that's a system, a game, or a good argument. I'm curious by nature and I enjoy learning new tools just to see what they can do.

This site is a small corner of the internet where I share a bit about myself and what I'm building. Feel free to look around, and reach out if something catches your eye.
